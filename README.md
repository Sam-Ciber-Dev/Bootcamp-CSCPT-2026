# Cybersecurity Challenge PT 2026: Final Selection Bootcamp (CSCPT 2026)

## Overview

Personal account of my participation in the final selection bootcamp of the [**Cybersecurity Challenge PT 2026**](https://cybersecuritychallenge.pt) (CSCPT 2026), a national talent-identification and development program in the field of cybersecurity, organised by the [Portuguese National Cybersecurity Centre (CNCS)](https://www.linkedin.com/company/centro-nacional-de-ciberseguran%C3%A7a---portuguese-national-cybersecurity-centre/).

The bootcamp took place from **17 to 20 July 2026 in Rio Maior**, bringing together **30 selected participants**. The first competition day was hosted on the international [**OMNI CTF**](https://omnictf.com) platform, where I competed alongside **786 participants worldwide**, and the second day was an internal CTF built by the program organisers.

This repository gathers my full participation report (Portuguese and English) and a photo record of the event. OMNI CTF also awarded me a **certificate of participation** for the OmniCTF 2026 Qualifiers.

## Scope

This is a first-person report describing my complete journey through the program, from the online training phase to the in-person bootcamp, including the results achieved at each stage and a critical assessment of the experience. It is a personal, documentary account and does not disclose challenge solutions or any restricted material.

The report was written by **José Samuel Oliveira** (Ph0enix) and is published for portfolio and documentation purposes.

## Program Phases

The program followed a structured progression, from an open online training phase through to the final in-person selection.

**1. Online Training Platform**

Registration was open between 19 February and 18 June 2026, with three training challenges released each week. This phase introduced the full variety of categories and exercises and served as consistent, months-long practice.

**2. Qualification CTF**

A jeopardy-style, online, individual CTF held on 20 June 2026, lasting 10 hours (09:00 to 19:00). This competition acted as the final filter, selecting the 30 participants for the in-person bootcamp.

**3. Bootcamp Day 1: OMNI CTF**

Competition on the international OMNI CTF platform against 786 participants worldwide, across categories such as MISC, PWN, Reverse, Forensics, Crypto, Games, Web and OSINT, within a daily time window followed by writeups.

**4. Bootcamp Day 2: Internal CTF**

A custom CTF built by the program organisers, covering Crypto, Reverse, Forensics, PWN and Web, followed once again by writeups of the chosen exercises.

**5. Results Day**

Announcement of the participants selected for the next phase, Team Portugal 2026.
